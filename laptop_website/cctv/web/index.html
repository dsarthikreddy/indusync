Unknown faces (photos saved as evidence)

How it decides

A face counts as a person when its similarity to that person's registered samples is at least the threshold (Settings, default 40%) and clearly better than any other person. A person must be recognised in 2 pictures within 3 s; an unknown face in 3 pictures within 5 s. Leaving = not seen for 20 s; coming back later = "re-entered".
